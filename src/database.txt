Independence Day:The day Algeria officially gained sovereignty from France ending 132 years of colonialism{1962}
The Setif Massacre:French colonial forces killed thousands of Algerians in Setif Guelma and Kherrata{1945}
The Battle of Algiers:Urban guerrilla warfare campaign by the FLN against French forces in the capital{1957}
The Evian Accords:Peace agreement signed between France and the GPRA ending the Algerian War{1962}
The Tripoli Programme:Ideological document adopted by the FLN defining socialist goals for independent Algeria{1962}
The Boumediene Coup:Military coup led by Colonel Boumediene that ousted President Ben Bella{1965}
Nationalisation of Oil:Algeria nationalized 51 percent of French oil companies giving control to Sonatrach{1971}
Black October Riots:Massive popular uprising in Algiers against the one-party system and economic hardship{1988}
The Constitution of 1989:New constitution that ended one-party rule and opened Algeria to multiparty democracy{1989}
The Cancelled Elections:Military cancelled the second round of elections after FIS won the first round{1992}
The Black Decade:Period of brutal civil war between the government and Islamist armed groups killing 200000{1991}
The Civil Concord:Peace initiative under Bouteflika offering amnesty to armed groups ending the civil war{1999}
The Arab Spring in Algeria:Algeria experienced protests but avoided the full uprising seen in neighboring countries{2011}
The Hirak Movement:Massive peaceful protest movement that forced President Bouteflika to resign after 20 years{2019}
Battle of Isly:French forces defeated Moroccan army at the Battle of Isly near Oujda securing Algeria{1844}
Capture of Emir Abdelkader:French forces captured the Emir after years of resistance ending organized armed opposition{1847}
The Mokrani Revolt:Major uprising led by Mohamed El Mokrani against French land seizures in Kabylie{1871}
The Margueritte Insurrection:Algerian uprising against settler land policies in the Ain Defla region{1901}
